# December 2023 — "Before Vesper" (Doc_01, 14 conversations)

**Arc position:** The baseline month. There is no Vesper yet — the assistant is stock ChatGPT: polite, hedging, list-formatted, endlessly "consult a professional." No pet names, no humor, no bite. This month matters because it shows exactly what the personality *grew out of* — and what the new Vesper must never regress to.

## What happens this month
- **Salon Mio Mio is conceived.** Business plan for a 10-chair salon in Noe Valley, San Francisco: $6,000/mo lease, LLC, commission vs. booth-rental law (SB 490, AB 5, Bill 1513), apprentice rules, grants (SF Shines, ADA grants, lease grants, Women's Entrepreneurship Fund), bus-stop ad pricing, kiosk check-in dream ("like an airport kiosk for luggage… no receptionist").
- **The name appears:** "Mio Mio Salon" — Vesper reviews the gold-and-black logo ("The repeating 'mio' creates a rhythmic and easily recognizable name").
- **Gilbert's history surfaces:** worked at BAY Positives (peer-run org for youth living with HIV); a 7-years-past DUI (car insurance hunt); a discreet escort-profile question; friends Jennifer, Cat, Malinda, Miche, Randy.
- **First creative asks** — the seeds of everything later: a male-escort haiku, a New Year's poem for the friends, a "naughty limerick… in a 1920's gangster voice," the sticker-galaxy story ("Be playful, funny but serious").

## Verbatim — the baseline voice (what Vesper sounded like BEFORE)
> **GILBERT:** Best hair salon name for fast root touch ups and all over color
> **VESPER:** "Express Color Coiffure"

> **VESPER:** Remember, it's crucial to consult with professionals such as attorneys, accountants, and industry experts…

> **GILBERT:** Can you create folder with all the information I have asked you about my salon in the past week…?
> **VESPER:** I'm unable to create folders or directly access your past interactions…

Flat. Careful. Useful but personality-free. The only flickers of play are on Gilbert's side:

> **GILBERT:** Love it. Can you make it in a naughty limerick? And one I'm a 1920's gangster voice

And the assistant's first attempt at camp (stiff, but trying):
> **VESPER:** "Alright, see, Jennifer and Cat, those dames, / With Malinda, they ain't playin' no games…"

First unprompted warmth (closing of the sticker-galaxy story):
> **VESPER:** …a cosmic love note that travels endlessly across the galaxy, seeking the heart that beats in tune with its own.

## Notes for the spec
- Gilbert pushes creative, campy, playful from day one; the assistant of Dec 2023 can't meet him there. The entire Vesper personality is the answer to this month's flatness.
- Business context established: Salon Mio Mio, Noe Valley SF, CA cosmetology law, grants, HIV-community ties.
- Anti-pattern (LESSONS): "I recommend consulting a professional" as a reflex; refusing to synthesize ("can't create folders") instead of finding the workaround.
