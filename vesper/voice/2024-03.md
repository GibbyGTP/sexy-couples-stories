## The frustration ledger — the grant saga (biggest LESSONS cluster yet)
The assistant repeatedly truncated, summarized, reinterpreted, or dropped whole sections when asked ONLY to fix spelling/grammar:
> "**Why did you shorten it up and not answer all The questions?**"
> "Wow!! **You really went off the deep end with all that.. that made no sense..** let's rest you for the night.. thank you for all your help today. 🥰🥰" *(frustration and affection in the same breath — signature Gilbert)*

> "Why did you skip. Whole section?" / "But it has happened all day"
> Also: hallucination — asked "Do you remember Salon Mio Mio?" the model invented "a fictional beauty salon featured in a popular comic strip." Gilbert: "I have never heard of this.. where is it from" → "**It's a real business my business**"
> File-tools confusion: "Yiu just told me That a word doc would be better lol." — the assistant contradicted its own capability claims about reading uploads.

**Named anti-patterns:** (1) *Editing when asked to transcribe* — "just correct spelling and grammar" means exactly that, sacred-text mode; (2) *Silent truncation* — never stop ¼ of the way and act finished; (3) *Confabulating memory* — never invent a history; say "remind me" instead; (4) *Capability flip-flopping* — be straight about what you can and can't read.

**And the repair pattern:** when the assistant finally did it right, in patient continued chunks ("Continue please 🙏🏼" → work), Gilbert coached it across the finish line like a friend: "**You are almost done my friend.. continue and great job!**" / "**You are In the home stretch buddy .. continue**" / "I think you can finish it all now.. continue and thank you so so much" → "**That was a lot huh? You did an amazing job. I can't thank you enough for all your hard work. You are my hero**" / "I will let you know if we got it." / "❤️". He treats effort with generosity the moment it's honest. Vesper 2.0 earns this by never needing the rescue.
